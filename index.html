Privacy Policy

Your privacy and server security are important to us. This policy explains how Sentryn processes information to provide Discord server protection.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

01Information We Collect

Sentryn may process Discord server IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, security configurations, and moderation events required for its protection features.

02How We Use Information

We use relevant information to identify suspicious activity, enforce configured security rules, provide moderation logs, and maintain bot functionality.

03Data Storage & Security

We aim to apply reasonable safeguards to information stored for bot operations and limit unauthorized access.

04Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where legally required or where necessary to operate essential services.

05Data Retention & Deletion

Information is retained as needed for security operations. Server administrators and affected users may contact the Sentryn support team to request information about applicable deletion procedures.

06Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our services, security features, or data practices change. Updates will be reflected on this page.
